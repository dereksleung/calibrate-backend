Calibrate · design confirmation

Day Log sync, private cache, and calendar-week navigation

The decision tree is complete. This review captures the agreed replacement for conditional GETs, the cross-tab privacy protocol, and the Logs experience before the ADR and task plan are amended.

All design decisions resolved
Read protocol

Delta sync, not ETags

POST /daylogs:sync accepts a consecutive range of at most 31 dates and known plain version numbers. It returns 204 when all slots match; otherwise it returns only changed or unloaded slots.

Private cache

Authorised by a durable fence

Day Logs restore only after confirmed identity. A generation fence, atomic IndexedDB transactions, resume checks, and fast BroadcastChannel delivery prevent a stale tab from recreating private cache data.

Logs UX

Calendar week outside, real days inside

The strip is Sunday–Saturday. Current-week sync is the Dashboard-compatible Seven-day view ending today; future cells are Upcoming. Selecting a stale historic day syncs that date and its six predecessors.

Mutation burst control

Writes patch locally—no follow-up sync

  • ✓Every response-visible Day Log write atomically increments a positive, server-owned versionNumber.
  • ✓Food Entry creation returns the entry, its Day Log ID, and its previous/new version numbers.
  • ✓A matching cached predecessor is patched and promoted to the new version without a second request.
  • ✓A mismatch is locally acknowledged but marked unverified; the next eligible normal range sync corrects it.
Cross-tab privacy

Logout is a cache revocation event

  • ✓Successful logout or confirmed session loss increments a durable cache-fence generation and deletes the matching cache record in one IndexedDB transaction.
  • ✓Every save/restore validates its captured account-and-generation lease inside its cache transaction.
  • ✓BroadcastChannel clears live tabs promptly; startup, focus, visible, pageshow, and a 15-second visible-tab loop cover missed delivery.
  • ✓Failed logout and transient connectivity/storage failures do not falsely clear authenticated data; unavailable IndexedDB becomes an empty online cache.

Lifecycle and consistency rules

Bounded, testable behavior
01 · Identity

Scope every private query

Query keys and persisted records include account identity. Before an account transition, private in-memory queries are cancelled and removed.

02 · Retention

Keep synced entries for 30 days

Explicit pruning by last sync time happens before hydration and persistence; it does not depend on a JavaScript timeout longer than its practical limit.

03 · Freshness

Synchronize only when eligible

Each date slot records its own last validation. A one-hour-stale or unverified selected date synchronizes in the background with its six predecessors. NutrientAnalytics syncs its 28-day view only on drawer open when stale. Offline/transient errors preserve visible stale cache and retry later.

Explicit boundary

What this can and cannot guarantee

Guaranteed after durable revocation:

No stale tab can successfully persist or restore a private cache record after it observes the newer fence generation.

Not physically instantaneous:

A frozen browser tab cannot be forced to erase already-painted pixels at the exact moment another tab logs out. The protocol clears active tabs promptly and rechecks before dormant-tab reuse.

Validation focus

Tests that prove the contract

  • ✓Projection-only unchanged sync, changed/null/unloaded slots, 31-day validation, account isolation, and atomic version advancement.
  • ✓Two-tab logout, missed broadcast + resume, stale-save race, account transition, and session-loss teardown.
  • ✓Calendar-week Sunday/DST/year boundaries; current-week reuse; selected historic-day rolling reads; Upcoming-state rendering; offline stale cache.
  • ✓Drawer-open-only 28-day sync, cache-first coverage messaging, pending Change analysis for partial history, and a latest-seven-days Total after 28 slots arrive.
